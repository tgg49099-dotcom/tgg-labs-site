Questions

How do I pay?
Reports and dossiers are paid by card through our checkout partner, which handles VAT. Licences are invoiced and paid in advance by bank transfer.
Do I need a call?
No. Questions, invoices and terms are handled by email.
What does the confidence level mean?
Each engine model is marked confirmed, make only, several makes allowed or unknown, so you see what the file actually says.
Do you sell the documents?
No. We sell facts drawn from official filings, each with its source link, page and date. The documents belong to their authors.
Prices and VAT
Prices exclude VAT. Business customers in the EU give their VAT number on the invoice.
